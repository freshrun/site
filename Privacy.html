FreshRun Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

FreshRun helps you discover and navigate fresh running routes while respecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, how it is used, and which third-party services are involved.

In short

  • FreshRun has no FreshRun account. It does not ask for your name or email address.
  • Your saved routes, runs and settings are stored on your device, and only copied to your own private iCloud if you choose to sign in with iCloud.
  • FreshRun's routing server receives only the points needed to build your route. Your live position during a run is never sent to it.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no AI is used to process your data.
  • FreshRun does not sell or share your personal data.

1. Overview

FreshRun is built to use as little personal data as possible.

  • FreshRun does not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • FreshRun does not use artificial intelligence or machine learning services to process your data. Routes are calculated by a routing engine, and personalisation is worked out on your device.
  • FreshRun does not access Apple Health, your contacts, your photos or your microphone.

2. Information We Process

Location Data

FreshRun uses your device's location to generate nearby running routes and to provide live navigation during a run.

  • Route generation. When you generate a route, the start point (your current location or a point you choose), any end point, and points around it that FreshRun picks to shape the loop are sent to FreshRun's routing server to calculate the route. If you start from your current location, this is your precise location at that moment.
  • Live Run. During a run, FreshRun processes your precise location and heading on your device to give turn-by-turn guidance and spoken splits. Your live position is not sent to FreshRun's servers.
  • Background location. Location is used in the background only during an active run, so that navigation continues when your screen is locked.

FreshRun does not keep a GPS track of your runs. A saved activity records its name, date, duration, the distance you ran, and which saved route it was run on, if any. FreshRun does not use your location for advertising or profiling.

Sign-In Options: Guest or iCloud

FreshRun has no email-based login. When you first open the app you choose one of two options:

  • Guest: the app works entirely on your device, with no sign-in of any kind.
  • Sign in with iCloud: gives you a higher daily route limit and backs up your saved routes and activities to your own private iCloud database, so they can be restored if you reinstall the app or change devices.

Before iCloud sign-in is turned on, FreshRun asks you to confirm your identity with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. This check happens entirely on your device using Apple's system authentication. FreshRun never sees your biometric data or your Apple Account password.

If you sign in with iCloud and sync is on, the following is stored in your private iCloud database for FreshRun:

  • your saved routes, including their coordinates, start and end points, and names;
  • your activities; and
  • the times of today's route generations, used to apply the daily route limit.

This data is end-to-end encrypted using a key stored in your iCloud Keychain, so neither FreshRun's developer nor Apple can read it. FreshRun's developer has no access to your private iCloud database. You can turn sync off or sign out of iCloud in the app at any time.

If the iCloud account on your device changes, FreshRun stops syncing and asks you what to do before any data on the device is added to the new account.

Note for users of earlier versions: earlier versions of FreshRun offered sign-in with an email address and a one-time code, provided by Auth0. FreshRun no longer uses email sign-in or Auth0. Email addresses collected through the previous sign-in system are being deleted and will not be used to contact you.

Routing Requests

FreshRun uses its own routing server, running OpenRouteService with OpenStreetMap map data, to generate routes and turn-by-turn directions.

A routing request contains the coordinates described above, the route type and distance you chose, and your route options (for example roads or trails, and flat or hilly). It contains no name, email address or device identifier. Routing requests are used only to return your route and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Like most web servers, FreshRun's server may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) to keep the service secure and working. These logs are not used to identify you or build a profile, and are deleted regularly.

Route Personalisation

You can rate saved routes with a thumbs up or thumbs down. FreshRun then favours new loops that resemble routes you liked, steers away from routes like those you disliked, and, if Prefer New Streets is on, leans towards streets you haven't run yet. All of this is worked out on your device from your saved routes and activities. Your ratings, history and preferences are never sent to FreshRun's routing server, and are not included when you share a route.

Challenges, Goals, Streaks and Heat Map

Your weekly goal, streaks, challenges, personal records and exploration heat map are calculated on your device from your saved routes and activities. They are not sent to FreshRun's servers.

Location Search and Street Names

When you type an address, choose a search result or drop a pin, FreshRun uses Apple's MapKit and Core Location services to look up addresses and place names. During navigation, FreshRun may also send points along your route to Apple to look up street names for spoken directions. These requests go to Apple, not to FreshRun's servers, and are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Voice Guidance, Live Activities and Reminders

Spoken directions and splits are generated on your device by iOS. During a run, FreshRun can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with the route name, distance, elapsed time and next turn. It is updated by the app on your device; FreshRun does not use push notifications to send it.

If you turn on the optional weekly reminder, FreshRun schedules a notification on your device for the day, time and distance you choose. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Shared Route Links

You can share a saved route as a link. The link contains the route itself, including its exact start and finish locations. Nothing is uploaded to a FreshRun server to create it. Because anyone with the link can see the route, FreshRun shows a warning before you share it. Links are created only when you choose to share, using Apple's share sheet, and the app or service you share through handles it under its own privacy policy.

When you open a route link someone has shared with you, the route is added to your device, and to your iCloud database if sync is on.

GPX Export

You can export a route as a GPX file. The file is created on your device from the saved route and contains its coordinates, name and, where available, elevation data. You choose where to save or send it using Apple's share sheet.

Connectivity and Security Data

Traffic to FreshRun's server is delivered through Cloudflare. When the app contacts FreshRun's server, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and request metadata for security, abuse prevention and service delivery.

The app also checks whether the routing server is online. These checks contain no personal data, identifiers or location information.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • On your device: saved routes, activities, ratings, goals and challenges, your sign-in choice, disclaimer acceptance, reminder settings, appearance and other preferences. If you use iCloud Backup for your iPhone, this data may be included in the device backup managed by Apple.
  • In your private iCloud database (only if you sign in with iCloud and sync is on): saved routes, activities and today's route-generation times.
  • On FreshRun's servers: FreshRun does not store your saved routes, activities or any account data on its servers. Only the short-lived technical logs described above are kept.

Clear All Data in the app deletes all FreshRun data on your device, deletes your FreshRun data from your private iCloud database (if you are signed in to iCloud), and returns you to the sign-in screen. Your device needs an internet connection to delete the iCloud copy. If the iCloud copy can't be deleted, FreshRun tells you and deletes nothing until you try again or choose to clear this device only.

Signing out of iCloud in the app turns sync off but does not delete data already backed up. Use Clear All Data to delete that too. You can also remove FreshRun's iCloud data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Deleting the app removes its data from your device but not from iCloud.

4. How We Use Information

FreshRun uses information only to run the app's features:

  • generating running routes;
  • live navigation, spoken directions and splits, and the Lock Screen Live Activity;
  • optional backup and sync to your own iCloud account;
  • applying the daily route limit; and
  • keeping the routing service reliable and secure.

FreshRun does not sell, rent or share your personal data for advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple (MapKit, Core Location, iCloud and CloudKit): map display, location search, address and street-name lookup, and optional iCloud sync.
  • OpenRouteService (HeiGIT) and OpenStreetMap: the routing software and map data used on FreshRun's own routing server.
  • Cloudflare: network security, performance and delivery of requests to FreshRun's server.
  • IONOS: hosting for FreshRun's routing server.

6. Your Choices

  • Change or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings. Features that need your location, such as live navigation, won't work without it.
  • Use FreshRun as a guest, or sign in with iCloud. You can switch at any time.
  • Turn iCloud sync on or off in the app, or sign out of iCloud without deleting your data.
  • Use Clear All Data to delete everything on your device and in your FreshRun iCloud data.
  • Turn the weekly reminder on or off in the app, and Live Activities or notifications for FreshRun in iOS Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

FreshRun is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, the law or our service providers. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
